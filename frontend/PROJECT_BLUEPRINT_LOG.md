## 📑 Core Documented Modules & Endpoints
1. `src/api/db.js` — Core database APIs (`dbPatients`, `dbVisits`, `dbInventory`, `dbSuppliers`, `dbPurchases`, `dbPatientLedger`, `dbSales`).
2. `src/pages/MedicalStorePOS.jsx` — POS checkout, Rx pad viewer, Tendered cash calculation, Barcode stickers generator, and K.B Software Watermark thermal receipt printing.
3. `src/pages/SupplierPurchases.jsx` — Distributor bills log, itemized stock receiving (Categories, Strength, Batch, Expiry), View Bill modal, and Supplier credit settlement.
4. `src/pages/MedicalStoreInventory.jsx` — Inventory listing & Add Product Form with Category & Strength selectors.
5. `src/pages/PatientProfile.jsx` — Timeline history, Prescription Lightbox, and Khata Balance Payment Receive Modal.
6. `src/pages/PatientsList.jsx` — Patients directory with Red Khata Due badges.
